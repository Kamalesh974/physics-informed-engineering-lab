How the two labs connect

01 · BondLab

Model

Build or import a bond graph, set parameters, inputs, initial and boundary conditions.

02 · BondLab

Derive

Causality is assigned automatically and the governing ODEs are derived symbolically.

03 · PINNSIM →

Transfer

The structured model and equations are saved to the project folder and sent to PINNlab.

04 · PINNlab

Train & simulate

The equations become PyTorch autograd residuals in the physics loss; watch the prediction live.
